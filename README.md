# OrgSync

OrgSync keeps your company's current decisions and responsibilities available across your AI work. People ask in the AI apps they already use, such as Claude Code, Claude Desktop and claude.ai, and get answers from the sources their company has approved, with a citation for each one. Nothing is shared with the company until the person approves it.

- Website: [orgsync.co](https://orgsync.co)
- Plugin for Claude: [orgsync-ai/plugins](https://github.com/orgsync-ai/plugins)
- Help: [orgsync.co/help](https://orgsync.co/help)
